I am currently a Research Assistant at the MIND Lab in the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, supervised by Prof. [Chen Zhen](https://franciszchen.github.io/). I received my first-class honors B.Sc. degree in Data Science and Big Data Technology, with a stream specialization in Biological Life science from the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK Shenzhen). I am honored that I am the awardee of one of the presidential graduate (top 0.1%) from the CUHK Shenzhen from my active research and leadership engagement. 

My research interest is in multimodal healthcare/clinical AI with a focus in traceability diagnosis. During my undergraduate degree, I am actively engaged in multiple research projects, with my most recent one is leading the creation of the BioinfoMCP framework for bioinformatics tools.
